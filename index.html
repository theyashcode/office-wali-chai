How it started

Our Story

Four chapters, one chai stall, and a plan that actually worked.

Chapter One

The Meeting

Yash, Sam and Yash crossed paths at Tech Altum Institute, Noida — three students figuring out the same classes, the same deadlines, and the same tired evenings.

Chapter Two

The Tapri

Their usual stop was the chai tapri right outside campus — small glasses, big conversations, and the one place where every plan somehow began.

Chapter Three

The Idea

One ordinary evening, over the same cutting chai, the idea struck founder Yash: what if that tapri feeling could reach every office desk?

Chapter Four

The Plan

The three of them turned that one conversation into a real plan — and started building Office Wali Chai, one cup at a time.

About us

A Tapri Story, Grown Up

Office Wali Chai began as a conversation between three friends at a roadside stall in Noida, and it still runs on that same energy. No shortcuts, no fancy syrups — just good tea leaves, real milk, and the kind of care that made that first tapri worth returning to every evening.

Today we bring that exact cup to your office, brewed fresh and delivered fast, so the comfort of a street-side chai break is never more than a few minutes away.
