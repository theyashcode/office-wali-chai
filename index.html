Our Story

Three friends Yash, Saim, and Yashwant met at Tech Altum Institute. After some conversations, we became good friends. Yash was very good at coding. Yashwant was weak in studies, but he was hardworking and always ready to learn. Saim was also good at coding, and he and Yash started doing combined studies. One day, while drinking a cup of tea together, Yash got a thought: “How do office workers enjoy tea in their busy schedules?” That one thought gave us an idea Office Chai. We decided to create a website for it. At first, we were scared, thinking whether it would work or not. But then we believed in ourselves and started building the Office Chai Website
