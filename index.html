FEW WORDS ABOUT US

More Than Just

a Cup of Tea

Office Wali Chai was born out of a simple frustration — why is good chai so hard to find at work? We started in 2020 with a single stall near a Gurugram office complex, and today we serve thousands of cups daily to offices, students and chai lovers across the city.

Every cup we make uses real milk, hand-picked spices and no artificial flavours — because your break deserves better than a teabag in hot water.

WHY CHOOSE US

What Makes Our Chai Different

  • Our Chai
    🍵

    Crafted with Real Ingredients

    We never compromise on quality. Every batch of chai is brewed fresh using whole spices — real cardamom, fresh ginger, cinnamon sticks and premium loose-leaf Assam tea. No shortcuts, no powder mixes, no artificial anything. Just honest chai the way your dadi made it.

    Order a Cup
  • Our Story
    🏪

    Started from One Stall, Now Serving Thousands

    It started with one small stall, one gas burner and one dream — to serve the best chai in Gurugram. Four years later, Office Wali Chai has become a daily ritual for hundreds of offices and thousands of chai lovers. We grew not through ads but through word of mouth — because good chai speaks for itself.
